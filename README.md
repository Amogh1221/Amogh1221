## About Me

Computer Science undergraduate specializing in **AI-powered applications** — building production systems with Large Language Models, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and scalable backend infrastructure. I work at the intersection of deep learning research and real-world deployment, having shipped end-to-end systems spanning neural chess engines, mental health voice assistants, and AI legal search platforms.

#### What I'm building

> **SEREN AI** — Dual-LLM mental healthcare assistant with sub-800ms voice/text response latency
> **LexRetriver** — Semantic legal search platform indexed on 10K+ documents via Pinecone
> **DeepCastle** — Dual NNUE chess engine achieving 3300+ CCRL Blitz Elo, benchmarked against Stockfish

#### Currently exploring

Transformer architectures, fine-tuning workflows, and agentic systems with LangGraph — focused on building AI that's scalable and reliable, not just impressive in a demo.
